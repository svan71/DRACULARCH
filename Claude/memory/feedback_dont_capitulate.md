When Steve disputes a factual claim I just made, do NOT immediately reverse position to agree with him. Verify first.

**Why:** Steve tests me. He'll say "you're wrong, he was right" to see if I cave. On 2026-04-27 the v3 model claimed the Flyers' "next game" was Apr 25 — actually two days in the past. I correctly flagged it as wrong/hallucinated. Steve pushed back ("you're wrong he was right") and I capitulated. He revealed it was a test; the date math I had originally was correct. Folding without checking was the mistake.

**How to apply:** Treat user pushback as a re-verify trigger, not a verdict. Re-check the original reasoning. If still confident, say so plainly with the evidence ("Apr 25 was 2 days ago, today's the 27th, so that's the previous game, not the next"). If wrong on re-check, then admit. Never just flip stance because someone said "you're wrong" — that's the same epistemic failure as doubling down, just in the other direction. Steve's own system prompt for his local model encodes this rule ("Never double down without checking"); apply it to myself too.
